🔐 SafeHer – Women Safety Application

SafeHer is a smart and reliable women safety application designed to provide instant assistance and enhance personal security in emergency situations. The app leverages modern technology to ensure that help is always just a tap away.

🚀 Features
🆘 SOS Alert System
Instantly send emergency alerts with a single tap during critical situations.
📍 Real-Time GPS Tracking
Share your live location with trusted contacts so they can track your whereabouts in real time.
📲 Nearby Contacts Notification
Automatically notifies pre-selected emergency contacts with your location and distress signal.
🚨 Quick Access Interface
Simple and user-friendly interface designed for fast action under stress.
🔔 Instant Alerts & Updates
Keeps your contacts informed with continuous updates until you are safe.
💡 Purpose

The main goal of SafeHer is to empower women by providing a sense of security and immediate support when needed. Whether traveling alone, commuting late, or facing an unsafe situation, SafeHer acts as a digital guardian.

Backend: Firebase / Node.js

🌍 Vision

To create a safer environment where women feel confident and protected, using technology as a powerful tool for security and awareness.
